# Perfect weeks — 12 Guys 1 Cup, 2021–2026

A **perfect week** is a team-week where the actual score equals the maximum score obtainable
from the players on that roster that week: the manager started exactly the right nine. This is
the figure Sleeper's weekly report surfaces, and it *can* be reconstructed from the public API.

Generated by `perfect-weeks.mjs` (2026-11-24, data through 2026 week 11).

```bash
node research/perfect-weeks/perfect-weeks.mjs
```

## How it works

Everything needed is in the public API, for every season back to 2021:

- `GET /league/{id}` → `roster_positions`. Identical all six seasons:
  `QB, RB, RB, WR, WR, TE, FLEX, K, DEF` + 17 bench, no IR slot. `previous_league_id` chains
  2026 → 2025 → 2024 → 2023 → 2022 → 2021.
- `GET /league/{id}/matchups/{week}` → `players` (the whole roster that week), `players_points`
  (points for **every** rostered player, bench included), `starters`, `starters_points`, `points`.
- `GET /players/nfl` → each player's `fantasy_positions`, for slot eligibility. Team defenses
  resolve by abbreviation (`LAR` → `DEF`).

The optimum is the app's own solver, `assignLineup` in `src/utils/lineupAssignment.js` — exact
maximum-weight assignment of players to slots (Hungarian), the same code behind the recap's
bench-points award. A week is perfect when `|optimal − actual| < 0.005`.

### Scope

- All weeks **1–17**, every team, every season: regular season, playoffs *and* consolation.
  The CSV carries `regularSeason` and `hadRealOpponent` so the number can be re-cut.
- **Week 18 excluded.** Past seasons do return scoring data for week 18, but every entry has
  `matchup_id: null` — the league had already ended and no lineup was being contested.
- **2026 counts weeks 1–11.** Week 11 is the last complete week; week 12 had not kicked off.
- Unplayed weeks are skipped automatically (every score zero), so a 0–0 week can't be scored
  as trivially perfect.

## Verification

1. **Reconciled against Sleeper's own numbers.** Summing weekly optima over the regular season
   should reproduce each roster's stored `settings.ppts` ("potential points"), and actual scores
   should reproduce `fpts`. 2022, 2025 and 2026 match **all 12 rosters to the cent**. The
   divergences are listed in caveat 1 and the script prints and classifies each one on every run.
2. **Independent second solver.** Every one of the 1,152 team-weeks was recomputed with a
   completely different method — position-count enumeration over which position fills the flex,
   no assignment algorithm. It never beat the Hungarian solver anywhere, and agreed on all 9
   perfect weeks. Its only two disagreements were its own fault: it reads one position per player
   and so mishandled Travis Hunter (`fantasy_positions: ['DB','WR']`), whom `assignLineup` slots
   correctly. Neither week was a perfect week.
3. **Hand-checked.** All nine lineups were inspected against their bench, and all four near
   misses were traced to the exact swap that would have been needed.
4. Still outstanding: open Sleeper's weekly report for one named week above (2026 wk 11,
   SMcenelly, is the freshest) and confirm it agrees. Sleeper's report is the thing that prompted
   the question, so it is the real acceptance test.

## Caveats

1. **Sleeper's stored season totals drift from its own weekly data in older seasons.** 2022,
   2025 and 2026 reconcile perfectly. 2024 and 2023 each have two rosters off, 2021 has six. Most
   are stat corrections applied to the weekly data after the season total was frozen — the actual
   total is off by the same amount as the optimum (e.g. 2021 ghowley: both +2.00), or only `fpts`
   drifted while the optimum matched `ppts` exactly (2024 Trav563, 2023 nlemieux24: +1.00). These
   do not affect perfect-week detection, which compares an optimum and an actual drawn from the
   *same* weekly payload.
2. **Three rosters where our optimum exceeds Sleeper's `ppts` while the actual matches exactly**
   (2021 JD0619 +26.00, 2024 JD0619 +38.92, 2021 Tookie23 +0.70). Our pool is the end-of-week
   roster, which can include a player who was not actually startable that week — on IR, or added
   after lineups locked. Sleeper excludes those from potential points; we cannot, because
   historical IR membership is not in the API (`roster.reserve` is only a current snapshot).
   Partial confirmation: excluding Joshua Palmer (on IR today, and swapped into our optimum in
   2024 weeks 4, 7, 12 and 14) accounts for 20.20 of that roster's 38.92 gap.
   **The direction of this error is safe for this question** — it can only make our optimum too
   high, i.e. miss a perfect week, never invent one. So **9 is a floor**; Sleeper's report may
   count a few more. The four near misses were checked individually and none is explained this
   way, so any extra would be a week we currently show as missing by more than a point.
3. A week counts as perfect even when the bench had nothing to offer. The "best player correctly
   benched" column makes that visible, and the note above separates the genuinely hard ones.

## Files

- `perfect-weeks.mjs` — the script. Caches the 14 MB player dump in the OS temp dir for 24h.
- `perfect-weeks.csv` — all 1,152 team-weeks: season, week, manager, team, actual, optimal,
  points left on bench, `perfect`, `regularSeason`, `hadRealOpponent`, best benched player.
- `summary.json` — tallies, the full starting nine for each perfect week, near misses, and the
  per-roster reconciliation against `ppts`/`fpts`.
